Day 1 – Der Brief
  Narration: Early morning in the hallway of a Viennese apartment: a woman in her late thirties in a cosy sweater, seen from behind, holds a yellowed antique envelope and a handwritten letter; on the back of the envelope a faint blind embossing breaks off at the edge of the paper; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sniffs the paper and wags, grey winter light falling through the frosted glass of the front door
  Resolution: Inside the lobby of a grand 1870s hotel on Vienna's Ringstrasse, stucco, marble and polished brass: a woman in a modern winter coat with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail has just stepped through the brass revolving door; behind them, through the glass, the very first snowflake drifts down under a street lantern. Behind a polished reception desk an elderly porter in an immaculate dark uniform looks up at them with a tired, knowing smile, as if he had been waiting for hours

Day 2 – Zimmer 24
  Narration: Lobby of a grand Viennese hotel during a heavy snowstorm, snow piling against the tall windows, the Ringstrasse outside white and empty, a tram standing still. An elderly porter in an immaculate dark uniform raises a hand toward a wooden key board with five brass hooks. A man in his thirties with a scarf takes a wet coat from a woman's shoulders, a dark green linen notebook under his arm. A slim woman in her fifties with a strict hair bun watches as a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail shakes snow off its coat onto a Persian rug and her polished shoes
  Resolution: Startbild: night, over the shoulder of a woman leaning on the gallery railing of a grand hotel, looking down into the dark lobby: an antique snow globe with a miniature grand hotel inside stands on a velvet cushion in a glass display case, faint gold glow, nobody near it. Bewegung: im Inneren beginnt es langsam zu schneien, obwohl niemand die Kugel berührt. Kamera steht still.

Day 3 – Die leere Vitrine
  Narration: Morning in a grand hotel lobby: an intact, locked glass display case, empty except for a velvet cushion with a circle in the dust where a snow globe stood; beside it a slim woman in her fifties with a strict hair bun, a hand at her mouth. On the reception desk a neatly folded porter's cap with a small slip of paper under it, a porter's coat still hanging on a hook behind. Through the glass entrance doors knee-deep untouched snow without a single footprint. A tall grandfather clock in the corner
  Resolution: Behind a hotel reception desk a narrow door, easily mistaken for a cupboard, stands ajar, warm light and rows of small brass compartments glimpsed inside; a small old postage stamp showing a grand lobby clock is tucked into the brass door handle, a woman's hand reaching for it, a slip of paper with scrambled letters in her other hand

Day 4 – Die Zeitpost
  Narration: A hidden room behind a hotel reception: walls of hundreds of small brass compartments engraved with dates, some doors open and empty, some green with verdigris. On a lectern in the middle an old ledger lies open, one page torn out roughly, leaving a jagged stub. A woman's hand holds a small UV lamp; under its violet light angular rune-like symbols glow on the page. Beside the ledger a card with angular characters and a handwritten note
  Resolution: Wood-panelled hotel library on the first floor, green banker's lamps: a man in his thirties with a scarf sits at a table covered in notes, looking up with a friendly smile, a dark green linen notebook beside him; a woman in a modern winter coat stands in the doorway; right next to the door a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail lies motionless in front of a bookshelf, staring at it

Day 5 – Sechs Alibis
  Narration: A wood-panelled hotel salon on a snowy afternoon, fire crackling: six people sit stiffly on chairs and sofas, faces half in shadow: a slim woman with a strict hair bun, a well-groomed man in his fifties, a man in his thirties with a scarf, a very old woman with silver hair, a young maid in a black uniform with a white apron, a broad-shouldered cook in a white jacket. In the best armchair by the fire, the one with velvet tassels, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sprawls comfortably. A folded handwritten night log on a side table
  Resolution: A young maid in a black uniform with a white apron hurries out of an elegant salon through a plain staff door; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sits in front of the closing door, looking after her; in the background a broad-shouldered cook in a white jacket stares at his hands

Day 6 – Constanzes Schulden
  Narration: An elegant hotel director's office in winter: a desk piled with unpaid bills and a wine list covered in pencil notes, behind it an old heavy safe with three brass number dials. A slim woman with a strict hair bun is just leaving through the door; a woman in a modern winter coat stands alone by the desk looking at the safe, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail beside her with its head tilted, snow outside the window
  Resolution: A woman's hands holding an official insurance policy taken from an open antique safe, a photograph of a snow globe clipped to it, a signature at the bottom; between the papers in the safe a small old Austrian postage stamp showing the rotunda of the 1873 Vienna World Exhibition; behind her the office door is just opening, warm lamplight

Day 7 – Miras Lüge
  Narration: A narrow servants' staircase lit by bare bulbs, worn wooden steps. On the steps a broken pearl necklace laid out deliberately in small groups of large and small pearls, separated by tiny golden knots. On the wall behind dusty glass an old framed telegraph Morse code chart. A brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail with a single pearl in its mouth looks back at a woman following up the stairs
  Resolution: A hotel kitchen at night, copper pots on the wall: a young maid sits at a wooden table, both hands around a cup; behind her a broad-shouldered cook in a white jacket rests a hand on her shoulder. Under the table a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail chews contentedly on a piece of sausage

Day 8 – Frau Lenz
  Narration: A glass winter garden in a grand hotel, palms and potted plants, snow outside the panes, afternoon light. A very old woman with silver hair and strikingly bright eyes sits in a wicker chair, an untouched cup of tea and a biscuit on a saucer beside her, an old leather photo album opening on the table. A brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail rests its head in her lap while she scratches it behind the ears; a woman in a modern winter coat sits next to her, seen from the side
  Resolution: Old black-and-white photograph with scalloped edges, a little girl in a wool coat in a snowy hotel courtyard next to a boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail, a woman's gloved hand at the edge of the frame, her face cut off

Day 9 – Die Marke im Glas
  Narration: A tiny porter's lodge in a grand Viennese hotel, barely bigger than a wardrobe: a wooden chair, a narrow writing desk, an old cast-iron radiator, a faded wall calendar from 1998. The bottom drawer of the desk is pulled open, full of receipts and pencil stubs. A woman in a modern winter coat, seen from behind, holds an old photograph up to the lamp light; it shows the glass base of a snow globe from below. In the doorway a well-groomed man in his fifties in a dark three-piece suit leans against the frame, an expensive camel overcoat draped over his forearm, a thin smile
  Resolution: Close-up of the back of an old photograph in a woman's hand, a small vintage Austrian postage stamp glued to it showing a grand hotel facade in falling snow, a round black postmark across one corner, the stamp just coming loose at its edge, a narrow wooden desk and an open drawer with receipts and pencil stubs blurred in the background, warm lamp light

Day 10 – Schritte in der Nacht
  Narration: A grand Viennese hotel lobby at three in the morning, almost dark, black-and-white checkered marble floor. A trail of small puddles of melted snow, evenly spaced footprints, leads from an empty glass display case across the lobby toward the grand staircase; some prints have already dried to faint damp rims. Behind the reception desk a young night porter in uniform sleeps with his head on his arms. In the foreground a woman in a modern winter coat holds a flashlight, its narrow beam on the footprints, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail on a short leash beside her, nose raised, pulling to the left
  Resolution: Night, seen from a dim hotel corridor: a room door half open, two small screw holes where the room number should be. In the doorway a well-groomed man in his fifties in a silk dressing gown, hair tousled, eyes puffy as if just woken. Behind him in dim lamp light the edge of a bed, a pair of leather boots under it glistening wet, a half-smoked cigar in a crystal ashtray on the nightstand. At the lower edge of the frame the head of a brown brindle boxer, ears back, growling quietly

Day 11 – Die Dienstbotengänge
  Narration: An elegant second-floor hotel corridor with numbered room doors and patterned wallpaper; a hidden door cut into the wallpaper stands open, revealing a very narrow dark servants' passage beyond. A young maid in a black uniform with a white apron leads the way in with a small flashlight, glancing back and whispering; a woman in a modern winter coat follows, shoulders drawn in; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail pulls ahead, nose to the old floorboards where small crumbs of fresh snow lie
  Resolution: A very narrow, dark hidden servants' passage between hotel rooms, old floorboards, bare plaster walls; at the far end a small door in the wallpaper has just swung shut, a thin line of light still under it. A young maid in a black uniform with a white apron stands frozen, a flashlight in her hand; its beam catches a faint wisp of cigar smoke hanging in the air. A woman in a modern winter coat behind her, holding a folded cream note; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sniffing the floor toward the closed door

Day 12 – Die Replik
  Narration: Two o'clock at night on the gallery of a grand hotel: a door hidden in the wallpaper has just opened, and a well-groomed man in his fifties in a long dark coat steps out, carefully carrying a wooden crate under his arm, and freezes; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail stands squarely in front of him, legs apart, growling; a woman in a modern winter coat, seen from behind, blocks the way; dim gallery lamps, the dark lobby below
  Resolution: Startbild: on the steps of a dimly lit hotel staircase two nearly identical antique snow globes with miniature hotels stand side by side, one still half in a wooden crate filled with wood wool, a woman holds an old photograph of a snow globe next to them; beside them a well-groomed man in his fifties in a long dark coat sits on the steps, head bowed, laughing joylessly. Bewegung: Kamera fährt langsam an der rechten Kugel vorbei, Schnitt auf eine Hotelzimmertür, unter der ein brauner Umschlag mit verblasstem Stempel durchgeschoben wird, Flurlicht fällt herein, ein Boxer schnuppert daran und wedelt.

Day 13 – Die Akte
  Narration: Night in a hotel room: a thin yellowed 1926 police file spread across the bed under a reading lamp, the cover with an eagle emblem and an illegible signature, typed witness statements each marked with a large red letter stamp; a blurred black-and-white photograph of a woman in falling snow, collar turned up, a boxer at her side, her face only a pale smudge; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail lies stretched out right on top of the papers
  Resolution: Close-up of a typed page from a yellowed 1926 police report, a witness statement by a page boy with a red letter stamp; in the margin a quick pencil sketch of two small circles side by side and next to them a single handwritten pencil word: "Zwei?" (the only text that should be legible); warm light from a bedside lamp, the paw of a boxer resting at the edge of the page

Day 14 – Tauwetter
  Narration: A small old glassblowing workshop in a snowy back courtyard in a Viennese suburb, between a carpenter's shop and a former coal cellar; inside, a glowing furnace and shelves of snow globes, Christmas baubles and glass lampshades. An elderly glassblower with burn scars on his hands carries a huge leather-bound order book with both hands; a woman in a modern winter coat has just taken off her scarf; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sits by the door, eyeing the hot furnace suspiciously
  Resolution: Close-up of an old order book entry in faded brown ink, order number 120, a pencil sketch of two identical snow globes, one with a small hollow drawn in its base; instead of a signature a faint blind-embossed mark pressed into the paper, a woman's fingertips touching it

Day 15 – Der Prunksaal
  Narration: The baroque state hall of the Austrian National Library in winter light: frescoed dome with painted gods, marble statues, leather-bound books rising to the vaulted ceiling. A woman sits alone at a reading desk with a slim leather-bound hotel chronicle open in front of her; a thin cardboard template with small rectangular cut-out windows and a small gold hourglass symbol in one corner has just slipped from the book into her lap
  Resolution: The back of a thin cardboard template with small rectangular windows, a small old Austrian postage stamp showing the Vienna Burgtheater stuck to it, lying on an open chronicle; through a tall window behind, far away over snowy rooftops, a hotel clock tower with a large milky clock face, both hands pointing to twelve

Day 16 – Unter dem Steffl
  Narration: Christmas market on Stephansplatz in Vienna at night, St. Stephen's Cathedral rising black into the sky, its colourful tiled roof under a thin layer of snow, rows of glowing wooden stalls with fairy lights, steam from roasting chestnuts. At a standing table a man in his thirties with a scarf raises a Christmas market mug, talking; a woman in a modern winter coat listens, her own mug untouched. Under the table a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail holds a star-shaped dog biscuit, watching the man
  Resolution: The Graben in Vienna late at night, giant glowing Christmas chandeliers hanging high above the empty snowy pedestrian street, the baroque Plague Column pale under the snowfall, a woman and a man with a scarf walking side by side but a little apart, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail pressed close against the woman's leg, both faces in shadow

Day 17 – Hietzinger Friedhof
  Narration: Hietzing cemetery under fresh snow at dusk, crows on old crosses, a forgotten corner with weathered graves and uncleared paths. A single trail of fresh, broad men's footprints leads to one old gravestone; in front of it a red grave candle burns. A woman in a modern winter coat kneels in the snow before the stone; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sits beside her with its nose resting on the stone. Bare trees and the cemetery wall in the background
  Resolution: Café Dommayer in Hietzing at dusk, a warm old Viennese coffee house with marble tables and bentwood chairs; a woman's hands around a cup of melange at a window table, snow falling outside; across the street a man with a hat pulled low watches her, half hidden in the snow flurries beside a street lamp

Day 18 – Stromausfall
  Narration: A grand hotel room during a power cut, almost completely dark: drawers torn out, the mattress shoved aside, the contents of a handbag scattered across the carpet. In the doorway a man in his thirties with a scarf holds a candle, looking concerned and out of breath; in front of him a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail stands rigid, growling low; a woman in a modern winter coat, seen from behind, in the middle of the room
  Resolution: A woman's hand pulling a small old Austrian postage stamp showing St. Stephen's Cathedral out of a coat pocket, a folded handwritten letter beside it, candlelight in a dark hotel corridor; in the background a young maid in a black uniform shaking her head as the electric lights flicker back on

Day 19 – Fannys Spur
  Narration: The Türkenschanzpark in Vienna on a snowy winter afternoon, the pond frozen, old lanterns already glowing, children sledging down a slope; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail pulls eagerly on the leash along the path, ignoring a snowman with a carrot nose, a woman in a modern winter coat following; beyond the park a lane of low houses leads uphill to vineyard slopes poking out of the snow
  Resolution: A deep candle-lit stone wine cellar under a closed Heuriger in Sievering, barrels and dusty bottles in the shadows; on a barrel an antique wooden gramophone with a brass horn, a shellac record on it whose paper label shows only the handwritten number "1224" (the only legible text); a candle and a small folded note beside it; a woman lowers the needle, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail pressed trembling against her legs

Day 20 – Der erste Helfer
  Narration: A dark green linen notebook lying open on a leather armchair by a frosted library window, a small gold crest of a tower between two stars on its spine, pages full of sketches of a clock tower, a floor plan and long lists of names with numbers; a folded yellowed ledger page with a roughly torn edge slipping out from between the pages, a woman's hand reaching for it
  Resolution: Startbild: the two halves of a torn ledger page joined on a library table, the line about Reiter now readable, the silhouette of a man with a scarf reflected in the dark window behind. Bewegung: Kamera hebt sich langsam vom Papier zum Fenster, die Spiegelung bekommt Schärfe.

Day 21 – Der Oberportier
  Narration: Morning light in a grand hotel lobby: an elderly porter with white hair in an immaculate dark uniform and cap stands behind the polished reception desk as if he had never been away; on the counter in front of him a brass cryptex cylinder with six letter rings; a woman in a modern winter coat with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail approaching the desk
  Resolution: At a hotel reception desk an elderly porter has just pulled a wool cloth off an antique snow globe with a miniature grand hotel inside, snow swirling in it; he has turned its glass base until a narrow compartment slid out, and in it lies a tiny ornate brass key, no longer than a finger joint; an opened brass cryptex cylinder and a small handwritten note beside it, a woman in a modern winter coat leaning in, warm lobby lamp light

Day 22 – Der Uhrturm
  Narration: Steep wooden stairs inside a clock tower: a woman carries a fully grown brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail up the last steps in her arms, the dog clearly complaining; above them an elderly porter with a lantern. At the top a vast, frozen clockwork the size of a wardrobe, brass and iron gears with a small ornate keyhole in the middle; behind it a huge milky clock face, and through it Vienna white with snow as far as the Kahlenberg
  Resolution: Startbild: inside a clock tower, giant brass and iron gears, a tiny ornate key in a small lock at the centre, behind it a milky clock face with snowy Vienna. Bewegung: die Zahnräder setzen sich langsam in Bewegung, die Zeiger hinter dem Zifferblatt rücken von zwölf weiter, hinter dem vierten Zahnrad springt eine kleine Klappe auf und eine gravierte Messingplatte schimmert, Schneeflocken treiben vorbei.

Day 23 – Das Archiv
  Narration: A dark wood-panelled hotel library just before midnight, lit by a single candle: a woman sits in front of the fourth bookshelf; a man in his thirties with a scarf steps out of the shadows between the shelves, no longer smiling; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail stands bristling between them; near the door a young maid, half hidden
  Resolution: A hidden archive vault behind a bookshelf: hundreds of small brass compartments engraved with dates; at the very back one compartment dated 24.12.1926 with a tiny padlock with three number rings; a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sits proudly in front of it; beside it an elderly porter holding a lantern and a woman in a modern winter coat; in the doorway a very old woman in a dressing gown with a candle, smiling

Day 24 – Der Brief vom 24. Dezember
  Narration: A grand hotel lobby on Christmas Eve, quiet and warm: a tall Christmas tree decorated with real burning candles; a young maid and a broad-shouldered cook stand side by side, openly holding hands; a very old woman with silver hair sits in an armchair by the fireplace, a blanket over her knees, looking toward the library; a slim woman with a strict hair bun lights the last candle; an elderly porter in uniform walks with a woman in a modern winter coat and a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail toward the dark library door
  Resolution: Startbild: a small writing desk in a candle-lit archive of brass compartments, a freshly written letter in an envelope whose blind-embossed mark slips off the edge of the paper, a feather pen, an embossing stamp, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail resting its head on the desk. Bewegung: die Kamera fährt langsam zurück, aus dem Archiv durch die Bibliothek in die Lobby, wo in der Vitrine die Kugel von selbst zu schneien beginnt, dann durch das Fenster hinaus, bis das ganze Hotel im verschneiten Wien bei Nacht zu sehen ist.
