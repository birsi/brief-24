Day 1 – Der Brief
  Narration: An aged envelope with a blind-embossed ex-libris stamp on a windowsill, snow falling over Vienna rooftops at dusk, the nose of a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail peeking into frame
  Resolution: Exterior of a grand 1870s hotel on Vienna's Ringstrasse at blue hour, illuminated facade and a glowing revolving door, the very first snowflakes drifting under an old street lantern, empty tram tracks in the foreground, a woman with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail small in the frame walking toward the entrance

Day 2 – Zimmer 24
  Narration: Grand hotel lobby during a snowstorm, an old concierge in front of a wooden key board with five brass hooks, a man with a scarf and notebook holding a wet coat, a woman with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail seen from behind
  Resolution: Startbild: an antique snow globe with a miniature grand hotel inside, in a glass display case in a dark hotel lobby at night, faint gold glow. Bewegung: im Inneren beginnt es langsam zu schneien, obwohl niemand die Kugel berührt. Kamera steht still.

Day 3 – Die leere Vitrine
  Narration: Empty glass display case in an opulent hotel lobby, a circle in the dust where a snow globe stood, a folded porter's cap on the reception desk, crystal chandelier
  Resolution: A narrow door behind a hotel reception desk standing ajar, warm light inside, an old postage stamp showing a lobby clock clamped to the brass handle

Day 4 – Die Zeitpost
  Narration: Wall of small brass post compartments engraved with dates, an open ledger with a torn-out page, a small lamp casting violet light
  Resolution: Wood-panelled hotel library with green banker's lamps, a man with a scarf writing at a table covered in notes, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail lying motionless in front of a bookshelf

Day 5 – Sechs Alibis
  Narration: Six people seated in a wood-panelled salon by a crackling fireplace, faces in shadow, snow outside the tall windows, a folded handwritten night log on a side table
  Resolution: Young maid in a 1920s-style uniform hurrying down a dim servants' corridor, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sitting in front of the corridor door, looking after her

Day 6 – Constanzes Schulden
  Narration: Elegant office desk with stacks of bills and a wine list covered in pencil notes, an old steel safe with three brass dials behind it, snow outside the window
  Resolution: An open antique safe with an insurance document and an old Austrian postage stamp showing the 1873 world exhibition rotunda, warm lamplight

Day 7 – Miras Lüge
  Narration: A single pearl on the worn wooden steps of a narrow servants' staircase, more pearls scattered further up, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sniffing at them, an old telegraph code chart framed on the wall
  Resolution: A young maid and a broad-shouldered cook holding hands in a dim hotel kitchen at night, a single candle between them, copper pots on the wall

Day 8 – Frau Lenz
  Narration: Very old woman with silver hair in a glass winter garden full of palms and snow outside, a teacup on a wicker table, an old leather photo album on her lap
  Resolution: Old black-and-white photograph with scalloped edges, a little girl in a wool coat in a snowy hotel courtyard next to a boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail, a woman's gloved hand at the edge of the frame, her face cut off

Day 9 – Die Marke im Glas
  Narration: Small cluttered porter's lodge with a brass key board, an old desk drawer pulled open, a faded photograph of a snow globe base seen from below, a man in an expensive tweed coat leaning in the doorway
  Resolution: An old Austrian postage stamp showing a grand hotel facade in falling snow, lying on a photograph of a snow globe, a magnifying glass beside it

Day 10 – Schritte in der Nacht
  Narration: Dark grand hotel lobby at night seen from a gallery above, a narrow flashlight beam, wet footprints of melted snow on black and white marble leading away from a glass display case
  Resolution: Pair of wet leather boots half hidden under a hotel bed, a crystal ashtray with a half-smoked cigar on the nightstand, dim lamp light

Day 11 – Die Dienstbotengänge
  Narration: Narrow hidden servants' passage behind wallpaper in a grand hotel, a young maid holding a candle lamp leading the way, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sniffing along the floor, a small wooden boot room at the end
  Resolution: Handwritten note with number triples on cream paper embossed with a gold crest of a tower between two stars, lying on a shelf in a dusty boot room next to an old shoe brush

Day 12 – Die Replik
  Narration: A man in a long dark coat stepping out of a hidden wallpaper door into a dim hotel corridor at night, holding a wooden box, a woman's silhouette and a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail blocking his way
  Resolution: Startbild: two nearly identical snow globes side by side on a dark table, one with a faint crack of gold light. Bewegung: Kamera fährt langsam an der rechten Kugel vorbei, Schnitt auf eine Hotelzimmertür, unter der ein Umschlag durchgeschoben wird, Flurlicht fällt herein.

Day 13 – Die Akte
  Narration: Thin yellowed police file spread on a hotel bed at night, a blurred black-and-white photo of a woman with a boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail in falling snow, red letter stamps on typed pages, a reading lamp
  Resolution: Margin of an old police report with a handwritten note in faded ink and a quick pencil sketch of two identical snow globes side by side, a question mark between them

Day 14 – Tauwetter
  Narration: Small old glassblowing workshop in a Viennese suburb, glowing furnace, rows of glass spheres on wooden shelves, an elderly craftsman opening a huge leather-bound order book, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail at the door
  Resolution: Close-up of an old order book entry in faded brown ink, order number 120, a pencil sketch of two identical snow globes, one with a small hollow drawn in its base

Day 15 – Der Prunksaal
  Narration: Vast baroque library hall with frescoed dome and marble statues, winter light through tall windows, a woman seated at a reading desk with a leather-bound hotel chronicle, a slim paper template with cut-out windows tucked between its pages
  Resolution: Old Austrian postage stamp showing the Vienna Burgtheater lying on an open chronicle page next to a technical drawing of clock gears

Day 16 – Unter dem Steffl
  Narration: Christmas market on Stephansplatz in Vienna at night, St. Stephen's Cathedral towering above glowing wooden stalls, light snow, a man with a scarf and a woman at a standing table holding steaming mugs, a brown brindle boxer dog with a long, natural, undocked tail at her feet
  Resolution: Two Christmas market mugs on a snowy standing table, one empty, one untouched and still steaming, a folded green linen notebook lying beside them, the Gothic portal of St. Stephen's Cathedral blurred in the background

Day 17 – Hietzinger Friedhof
  Narration: Snowy old cemetery at dusk, a weathered gravestone with a single lit red grave candle, footprints in fresh snow leading to it, the silhouette of Schönbrunn Palace in the distance, a woman with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail standing before the stone
  Resolution: Warm old Viennese coffee house at dusk, a woman's hands around a cup of melange at a window table, snow falling outside, across the street a man in a dark hat watching, half hidden behind a lamppost

Day 18 – Stromausfall
  Narration: Ransacked grand hotel room in total darkness, drawers pulled out, mattress askew, papers scattered on the floor, a narrow beam of violet light cutting across the wallpaper, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail standing tense in the doorway
  Resolution: Old Austrian postage stamp showing St. Stephen's Cathedral lying on a faded wine label in candlelight, the label reading a family wine estate in Sievering

Day 19 – Fannys Spur
  Narration: A brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail pulling on a leash along a snowy park path at dusk, old lanterns glowing, a frozen pond, paw prints in fresh snow leading uphill toward vineyard slopes
  Resolution: Antique wooden gramophone with a brass horn in a candle-lit stone wine cellar, a shellac record spinning, label reading recording number 1224, wine barrels in the shadows

Day 20 – Der erste Helfer
  Narration: A dark green linen notebook lying open on a leather armchair by a frosted library window, a gold crest of a tower between two stars on its spine, pages full of sketches of a clock tower and lists with prices, a folded torn ledger page sticking out
  Resolution: Startbild: the two halves of a torn ledger page joined on a library table, the line about Reiter now readable, a man's silhouette reflected in the dark window behind. Bewegung: Kamera hebt sich langsam vom Papier zum Fenster, die Spiegelung bekommt Schärfe.

Day 21 – Der Oberportier
  Narration: Elderly hotel concierge in an immaculate 1920s-style uniform standing behind a brass reception desk in morning light, a brass cryptex cylinder with letter rings lying in front of him, a woman with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail approaching
  Resolution: Old concierge placing an antique snow globe into a woman's hands, the glass base opened to reveal a tiny ornate brass key nestled inside, warm lobby lamp light

Day 22 – Der Uhrturm
  Narration: Steep wooden stairs inside a clock tower leading up to a vast mechanism of brass gears, a translucent clock face glowing with winter light, snowy Vienna rooftops visible through it, an old man with a lantern and a woman with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail climbing
  Resolution: Startbild: giant brass clock gears inside a tower, a tiny ornate key in a central lock, frosted clock face with Vienna behind. Bewegung: die Zahnräder setzen sich langsam in Bewegung, die Zeiger hinter dem Zifferblatt rücken von zwölf weiter, Schneeflocken treiben vorbei.

Day 23 – Das Archiv
  Narration: Dark wood-panelled library at midnight lit by a single candle, a tall bookshelf beginning to swing open like a door revealing brass compartments behind it, a man with a scarf stepping out of the shadows, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail bristling in front of a woman
  Resolution: Hidden archive vault of hundreds of small brass compartments engraved with dates, one compartment marked 24.12.1926 with a tiny three-ring padlock, a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail sitting proudly in front of it, an old concierge holding a lantern

Day 24 – Der Brief vom 24. Dezember
  Narration: Grand hotel lobby on Christmas Eve, a tall decorated tree with real candles, a small group gathered quietly: an elderly concierge, an owner with a strict bun, a young maid holding a cook's hand, a very old woman in an armchair, a woman with a brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail walking toward a dark library door
  Resolution: Startbild: the antique snow globe on a writing desk beside a sealed envelope with a blind-embossed mark, candlelight, a sleeping brown brindle boxer with a long, natural, undocked tail. Bewegung: in der Kugel beginnt es zu schneien, die Kamera fährt langsam zurück, durch das Fenster hinaus, bis das ganze Hotel im verschneiten Wien bei Nacht zu sehen ist.
